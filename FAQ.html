Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our library services

General Questions

How do I become a member?

To become a member, simply visit our registration page and fill out the required information. You'll need a valid email address and government-issued ID for verification.

What are the library hours?

Our library is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on public holidays.

Borrowing Books

How many books can I borrow at once?

Regular members can borrow up to 5 books at a time for a period of 14 days. Premium members can borrow up to 10 books for 21 days.

Can I renew my borrowed books?

Yes, you can renew your books up to 2 times, provided there are no holds on the items. You can renew online through your account or by visiting the library.

Digital Services

Do you offer e-books?

Yes, we have a growing collection of e-books available through our digital platform. You can access them using your library card number and PIN.

How do I access online resources?

All online resources are accessible through your library account. Simply log in with your credentials to access our digital collection, research databases, and more.
